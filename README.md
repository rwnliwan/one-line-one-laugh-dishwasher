Entry for the [One Line, One Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-coral-404-one-line-one-laugh-22f2c9f3) competition on make.fun: one funny line of at most 200 characters, judged pairwise by a comedy-editor judge.

## The line

> My dishwasher outsourced its job to me.

39 characters. The appliance quietly delegates its own work to its owner — a dishwasher that leaves the dishes dirty has not broken down, it has outsourced the washing to a human, and the human does it without a raise.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$ENTRY_OUTPUT` when set). Nothing else is submitted: the line is the whole entry.
